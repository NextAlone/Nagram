## Rules for modified and third-party distributions

Unless prior written permission has been obtained from the applicable rights
holder, a modified, forked, or third-party distribution must:

1. remove or replace the Nagram name, logos, and application icons used as
   product branding;
2. use a clearly distinct application name and icon;
3. remove Nagram brand assets from launcher icon selectors, manifests, release
   packages, app-store listings, screenshots, repository avatars, and other
   promotional material; and
4. not imply affiliation with, endorsement by, or official status from the
   Nagram project.

Accurate and non-confusing textual statements such as "based on Nagram" are
permitted. Copyright notices, source history, and other notices required by
the GPL must remain intact.
